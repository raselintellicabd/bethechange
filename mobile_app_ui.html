Diabetes

Naturopathic and integrative medicine can help prevent and manage diabetes using holistic therapies that support metabolism and blood sugar balance.

Common symptoms

FatigueIncreased thirstWeight changeBlood sugar swingsSlow healing

Factors that contribute

High-sugar diet, nutritional deficiencies, lack of exercise, stress, hormonal imbalances, and environmental toxins.

Our integrative approach

We focus on dietary and lifestyle changes that minimize your need for insulin, using testing to find underlying metabolic disruptors.

Benefits of integrative medicine

Blood sugar balanceSustained energyFewer medicationsWeight management
